How a film gets made

You see the look before a single second is animated.

  1. Brief & script

    One clear message about your product, turned into a script timed to the music.

  2. Style frames

    Key frames of the look: colour, type, 3D and UI. You approve them first.

  3. Animation & sound

    Every scene locked to the music, with real UI and sound design.

  4. Delivery

    Final film with sound, ready to post, plus a still for the thumbnail.
